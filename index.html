Your journal stays yours

There's no account to create. Your entries are stored on your device, so we don't hold them and can't read them. If you turn on backup or sync, your data goes to your own Google Drive or iCloud.

We do collect some data to run the app, including analytics, crash reports and device identifiers used for analytics and advertising. The privacy policy has the details.

Common questions

Is Gratitude free?

Yes. It's free to download and use. Gratitude Pro is an optional subscription, and the yearly plan includes a free trial for eligible users.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone and Android. The journal isn't available on the web, but the vision board maker runs in your browser.

Do I need an account?

No. You can start writing as soon as you open the app.

What happens if I change phones or delete the app?

Your data lives on your device, so deleting the app without a backup deletes your entries. Backup to Google Drive is free on iPhone and Android, and iCloud sync is available on Apple devices.

How is it different from other gratitude apps?

It keeps your journal, affirmations and vision boards together, so you don't need a separate app for each.
