Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Gratitude App

📝 Introduction

At Gratitude (Hapjoy Technologies Pvt. Ltd), we believe in transparency and honesty. We hope you will find this privacy policy clear and transparent. Do not hesitate to reach out to us at team@gratefulness.me if you have any questions.

📲 What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?

  • The Application does not provide an option to create an account therefore, we neither store nor have access to your entries and data.
  • Your data is stored only locally on your device and all the data processing needed to create insights and calendar views is done locally on your device.
  • You can optionally back up your data to Google Drive on both Android and Apple devices.
  • You can also opt-in to sync your Journal entries, Affirmations, Vision boards, Bookmarks, and Challenges progress across your Apple devices using the inbuilt iCloud Sync feature provided by Apple using your Apple ID.
  • Backups of your App data are stored in your private cloud space and we or any third parties don't have access to your backup files.
  • We obtain your email address when you request support via email or customer support contact form. We use your email only to contact you to provide support, feedback, or important information.

💾 Backup

  • You can optionally back up your app data using your Google Drive account.
  • We use a secure connection to your Google Account and data is safely stored in a folder that cannot be accessed by us or any third parties. View Google Privacy Policy at https://www.google.com/policies/privacy/
  • During backup, we ask for your email address. We back up your app data to the Google Drive account associated with your email address.
  • The app asks Google Sign In for a connected account and displays the account info only when accessing the Backup section.

📤 Export

  • You can optionally export your journal entries as a PDF and save at any place inside your phone or share them outside of the app via third parties like Gmail, WhatsApp, etc. Once exported we are not responsible for your exported files.

💳 In-app Purchases

  • Hapjoy Technologies Pvt. Ltd built the Gratitude Journal app as a freemium service. We provide auto-renewable in-app purchases inside the app. If a Pro plan is purchased the subscription will automatically renew unless turned off in your iTunes Account Settings (iOS users) or Google Play account settings (Android users) at least 24 hours before the current period ends.
  • We store no card information on the users inside the app. Your iTunes account (iOS users) or Google Play account (Android users) will be charged when a purchase is confirmed.
  • We also provide free trials on our Pro plans inside the app. If you are to opt-in for a free trial, you won't be charged any amount till your free trial expires. Your subscription will automatically start once the free trial duration ends. If you subscribe before your free trial ends, the rest of your free trial period will be forfeited as soon as your purchase is confirmed.
  • If you want to cancel your free trial, make sure to cancel it from your iTunes Account Settings (iOS users) or Google Play account settings (Android users) 24 hrs before the free trial ends.
  • We are not supporting family sharing of any In-App purchases in the app.

➡️ Share

  • Your data is stored locally on your device by default. This means if you delete the app, all your data will be lost unless you have backed up your data to your Google Drive account or exported them locally in PDF format.
  • We don't store or share any of your Journal entries or your app data in any format remotely. But, as a user, you can share your entries with your friends, family, and colleagues via third parties like WhatsApp, Instagram, messages, etc. We don't take any responsibility for the data shared by you.

🧘‍♂️ Mindful Minutes

  • The application logs Mindful minutes (you spend in the app) into the Health App with your consent. We do not use this data or share it with any 3rd party. You can always opt out to log mindful minutes from the settings page.

📊 Analytics

  • The application never sends your journal entries, affirmations, vision boards or any other content you write to us or to the services below.
  • The application collects some usage and crash data that we need to deliver our services, to understand your needs and to improve the app. For example, it collects how much time people spend on certain screens or which features are popular. The analytics services automatically collect certain information that does not personally identify you.

🔧 We use the following third-party services for our app:

Mixpanel

This service helps us to collect and analyze usage data to understand usage patterns and to improve our Android and iOS apps. Personal data collected: various usage and device data such as cookies, unique device identifiers, usage data, and anonymized IP addresses.

Firebase Crash Reporting (Google Inc.)

This service helps us to identify crashes and errors in our app. It helps us to improve the reliability and stability of our Android and iOS apps. Personal data collected: Instance IDs, Crash traces.

Firebase (Google Inc.)

This service helps us to send Daily Zen notifications to our Android users who have opted-in to get the daily notification at 08:00 AM on their devices.

OneSignal

This service helps us to send Daily Zen notifications to our iOS users who have opted-in to get the daily notification at 08:00 AM on their devices.

RevenueCat

This service manages Pro subscriptions and free trials in our Android and iOS apps. Personal data collected: an anonymous app user ID, purchase and subscription history, device and app version, country, and IP address.

Meta (Facebook SDK and Conversions API)

We advertise the app on Instagram and Facebook. To understand which of these ads lead to installs, free trials and subscriptions, the app uses the Facebook SDK, and RevenueCat tells Meta when a trial or subscription starts or renews. Personal data shared with Meta: that the app was installed or opened, trial and subscription events with their price, an anonymous Meta ID for your device, the identifier for vendor (IDFV) on iOS, device and app information, and IP address.

We don't ask for permission to track you on iOS, so the app does not collect your advertising identifier (IDFA) or link this data with other companies' apps and websites. On iOS, Meta measures ads through Apple's privacy-preserving SKAdNetwork and Aggregated Event Measurement. We don't send any of your journal content to Meta.

📖 Ordinary Gold journal reservations

This section covers the reservation page for our printed journal at gratefulness.me/ordinarygold. It is separate from the app, and nothing you enter there is connected to your journal entries.

What we collect when you reserve: your email address, name, country and number of copies. If you choose to give them, also your phone number and anything you write to us. We also record the price shown to you, the time, and how you found the page, such as the link or website you came from.

How we use it: to send a confirmation, write to you about this book, answer your questions, and understand how many people want it. We use your phone number only to reach you about your copy. We don't sell your details or use them to promote anything else.

Where it is kept: in Google Forms and Google Sheets in our Google Workspace account, which our team can access. The confirmation email is sent through Gmail. View Google's Privacy Policy at https://www.google.com/policies/privacy/

How long we keep it, and your choices: we keep it for as long as we need it for this book. To see, correct or delete your details, or to stop hearing from us, write to team@gratefulness.me.

Website analytics: this page uses Google Analytics to understand visits, such as which sections are seen, which buttons are clicked, your general location and device, and where you came from. Google Analytics uses cookies for this. The page also saves on your device how you first found it, so a later reservation can be credited correctly. We don't send your email address, name or phone number to Google Analytics.

🗑️ Deleting your data

  • Your journal data lives on your device and in your own iCloud or Google Drive backups. Deleting the app removes it from your device. You can delete backups from your iCloud or Google Drive account at any time.
  • The app has no account with us, so there is nothing else to close. To ask us to delete the usage, subscription or advertising data described above, email team@gratefulness.me. We will help you find the data linked to your device, delete what we hold within 30 days, and ask RevenueCat, Mixpanel and Meta to delete their copies.
